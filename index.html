About Us

Welcome to AmitQuizX

AmitQuizX is a premium educational platform designed to help students and knowledge seekers test their skills in General Knowledge, Science, History, and Geography. Our platform offers a unique interactive experience with thousands of questions.

How it works?

Simply choose a category from the home screen, select a section (each containing 50 challenging questions), and start playing. You can track your score in real-time and see your performance percentage at the end.

Help & Support

How to play?

Click on any category card, then select a section. Answer the multiple-choice questions within the 15-second timer.

Can I use it offline?

Yes! Once loaded, AmitQuizX works offline as a PWA. You can also install it on your home screen.

Privacy Policy

At AmitQuizX, we respect your privacy. We do not collect any personal information. Your quiz scores are stored locally on your device and are never shared with our servers.
